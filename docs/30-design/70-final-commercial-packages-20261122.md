Karda uses the same prices/seats/storage/connections, AI300/1200/4000 and
libraries200/1000/5000, with no commercial content-item cap. All published
services share the monthly call pool; publishing a service does not create a
separate call grant. External source data is not Arda hosted storage.

Enterprise is private deployment/contact sales, quotas by contract. Free remains
an internal design, not public sales. Implemented individual core capabilities
are open across tiers; organizational collaboration starts at Business. All
tiers support human assistance and tickets. Arda AI tasks are not yet delivered;
the final allocation must not be marketed as proof of shipped capabilities.

Final addon design:10GiB storage99CNY/month,1000AI credits200CNY/pack. Expiry
and audience follow the accepted platform contract. Addons do not participate
in the95% joint base price or20% campaign coupon. One coupon per order, first
paid or explicitly designated campaign cycle; no perpetual80% renewal discount.
Same-tier joint totals after both discounts:302.48/758.48/2278.48CNY/month.
Annual billing pays ten monthly list prices for twelve months of service.
Monthly AI/call grants must reset monthly; platform acceptance remains pending.
All four tiers use public subscription; Enterprise retains contact sales rather
than a zero-price self-service purchase. Free is excluded from new publication.
Tax/invoice treatment remains an unresolved commercial acceptance item.

Annual same-tier joint totals after95% joint pricing and one20% coupon are
3024.80/7584.80/22784.80CNY. The model budgets twelve months of costs against
ten months of list revenue. Base contribution margins are56.55/48.69/37.91%;
stress margins33.94/18.95/3.15%. These exclude tax and acquisition costs and
are not net profit. Business has little headroom in the full-discount stress case.

Storage is one actual workspace pool, AI follows reserved/shared policy, seats
remain product-specific. Do not duplicate displayed balances or infer10 shared
seats from two Business subscriptions. Egress is a cost scenario, not a new
published quota/fee. No guaranteed task counts, margins or unlimited compute.

The current published offer remains documented in50-commercial-plans.md.

then update drafts with readback evidence. Existing subscriptions stay pinned.
